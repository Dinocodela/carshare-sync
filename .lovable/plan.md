## Why these changes
- Removes guaranteed earnings figures ($1,500–$3,500) from headline claims — retained only as "based on host results, actual earnings vary" disclaimer language.
- Replaces absolutes ("zero effort", "literally everything", "100%", "ever", "always on time", "fully insured", "total peace of mind") with accurate, qualified verbs.
- Insurance line now attributes coverage to platforms (Turo/Eon) rather than implying Teslys-provided insurance.
- Payout language reflects that deposits depend on cleared funds, not an unconditional promise.
